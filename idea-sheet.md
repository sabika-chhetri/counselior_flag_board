# Idea Sheet — SA Wellbeing Platform

**Users:** (1) School counsellor — sees student answers, decides who to meet. (2) CECD/MoESD supervisor — needs patterns, never identities. (3) Students — answer honestly only if they trust privacy. (4) Principal/admin — needs to know it's happening, not the details.

**Top 3 pain points**
1. Counsellor can't tell who to see first among dozens of answer sheets.
2. No record of who has been met, so students can be missed or double-handled.
3. Reporting upward is manual, and risks exposing identities or hides patterns across schools.

**Ideas for a first feature**
1. Counsellor flag board: ranked list with reasons (picked)
2. Online survey form with mobile/offline mode
3. Follow-up tracker with reminders ("not seen in 7 days")
4. Supervisor dashboard with small-group suppression
5. Comment scanner for worrying words (English/Dzongkha)
6. SMS/WhatsApp alert to counsellor for Urgent answers
7. Year-on-year trend per class

**Pick: flag board + follow-up status.** It's the step where a child actually gets helped; the survey and reports are useless if no one acts. It's small, works on the data we have, and the rest (tracker, supervisor view) builds on the same flags.

**One thing I'd challenge:** the baseline rules treat "Prefer not to say" on the self-harm question like "No". A child who declines to answer is not safe by default, so I flag them as Review (High with other signals). Also, group size <5 hides small groups, but totals can reveal them by subtraction.
